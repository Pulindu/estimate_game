🫙 Bead Jar Guessing Game

Look at the jar! It is filled with 1,000 beads in 5 different colors. Each color has a different number of beads, and when we add them all together, we get exactly 1,000.

👋 About you

First, tell us your grade.

1️⃣ Which color do you think has the MOST beads?

Look at the jar and make your best guess. Tap a color.

2️⃣ Let's test your guess with a scoop!

Scoop out some beads from the jar. Does your scoop agree with your guess?

3️⃣ Count your scoop!

How many beads of your chosen color are in your scoop?

4️⃣ Time to estimate! 🔴

Look at the numbers in your scoop. If the whole jar has 1,000 beads, about how many red beads do you think there are?

Thanks for playing the Bead Jar Guessing Game! 🫙
